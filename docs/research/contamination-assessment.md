# Contamination assessment

`archguard.contamination-assessment/v1` records issue-solution clues, revealing commit messages,
documented corpus exposure, leaderboard/tutorial visibility, co-located gold, oracle artifacts,
duplicates and cross-split similarity. Risk is `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, or `UNKNOWN`; it is an
assessment, never proof that training contamination exists or is absent. Canary detection is high
risk and excluded. Other high-risk handling must be preregistered; unknown stays unknown.

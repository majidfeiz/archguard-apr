# Literature search protocol

## Scope and questions

Search primary work on APR; LLM repair; agentic software engineering; repository-level reasoning;
repository/program dependency graphs; architecture conformance; retrieval-augmented code generation;
execution-feedback repair; sandboxed execution; benchmarks; and reproducibility.

## Sources and query process

Search ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, SpringerLink, ScienceDirect, DOI records, official conference
proceedings, and original arXiv records. Combine each topic with “automated program repair”, record the
query date, deduplicate by DOI/title, and follow backward/forward references only after primary-record
verification. Current access date is 2026-10-14.

## Inclusion and exclusion

Include original peer-reviewed papers and primary benchmark/system papers directly relevant to the
research questions. Exclude unverifiable metadata, secondary-only claims, non-technical marketing,
duplicate versions, and works whose relevance cannot be established from the primary record.

## Verification policy

`VERIFIED` requires matching title, authors, year, venue, and DOI or official URL on a primary record.
`NEEDS_VERIFICATION` is a search lead and must not be cited. Never infer missing pages, author order,
venue, DOI, citation count, or results. The search supports “to the best of our documented search” only
after a human completes and signs off the matrix.

## Synthesis rules

Separate reported author claims from independently established facts; report benchmark and evaluation
scale; capture limitations; do not claim absence of prior work. Final relevance, novelty positioning,
and prose require human approval.

# Claim–evidence traceability matrix

The canonical matrix is [`claim-evidence-matrix.csv`](claim-evidence-matrix.csv). Eleven audited claims
cover repository analysis, graphs, explicit architecture constraints, patch security, retrieval,
sandboxed feedback, role-based orchestration, providers/cost, reproducibility, and the exploratory
result. “Implemented” means exercised by the cited code and tests; it does not imply empirical
effectiveness. Moderate-strength claims require especially careful qualification.

The eleventh claim constrains related-work positioning to 23 primary records independently verified
in the literature matrix; it permits no priority or first-system claim.

The Phase 13 effectiveness statement is limited to: **no observed effectiveness difference in two
valid paired tasks**. Prohibited extensions include superiority, significance, generalization,
production readiness, successful repair, or validation of complete autonomous multi-agent APR.
